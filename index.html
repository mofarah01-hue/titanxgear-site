The hardest work in the room is the part nobody can see.

Titan X puts every wrestler's heart rate and head contacts on one screen during practice, so the coach can act while it is still happening.

The room

One coach. Thirty athletes. Thirty different physiological states, and no way to see any of them.

The app

A practice, from plan to the season.

  1. Before
  2. During
  3. After
  4. Season
  5. Any time

  1. 1 Before practice

    Build the plan.

    Break practice into blocks and give each one a target heart rate zone: an easy warm-up, hard live rounds. A change for one athlete, like drilling instead of live wrestling on a sore shoulder, stays with that athlete.

  2. 2 During practice

    Run the floor.

    Every athlete on one screen: heart rate as a share of their max, the target for this block, and whether they are on it, in words as well as colour. Head contacts are counted as they happen. One tap pulls an athlete out.

  3. 3 After practice

    See what happened.

    Time on target for each athlete, who is worth a word, and head contacts by round. Add your call on tonight and a voice note, and it is saved.

  4. 4 Over the season

    Watch the trend, not the noise.

    Each athlete is compared with their own usual range. When heart rate starts dropping faster after a live round, the change is shown against that range, so a real improvement stands out.

  5. 5 Any time

    Ask in plain words.

    Who can go hard tonight? Write me a practice for Thursday. The assistant answers from your own athletes and plans, gives the reason for each one it leaves out, and nothing changes until you tap Apply.

The sensor

The best sensor is the one the athlete doesn't have to remember.

Headgear is on in every wrestling practice, because the sport requires it. A sensor inside it reads the heart signal and head motion from the same spot, so one device gives heart rate and head contacts.

Heart rate
Optical, from the head.
Head contacts
Counted from motion, per round and per practice.
Other sensors
Athletes can also wear a Polar heart rate sensor.
